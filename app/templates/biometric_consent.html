Biometric Search Consent

Facial search is voluntary. A customer can browse or contact the photographer without using this feature.

What the customer authorizes

By checking the consent box and submitting a selfie, the customer authorizes the platform to analyze facial features from that selfie and compare them only with faces indexed in the selected event gallery for the purpose of locating possible photographs of that customer.

Purpose limitation

The selfie and temporary facial representation are not intended for surveillance, background checks, advertising profiles, law-enforcement identification, or training a general facial-recognition model.

Retention

The submitted selfie should be deleted immediately after the search. Any temporary facial representation should be discarded when matching is complete. Event-photo indexes remain connected to the event until the photographer removes the gallery or the applicable retention period ends.

Accuracy and choice

Results may include false matches or miss photographs. Customers should review results before buying. Consent may be declined, and a customer can instead contact the photographer for assistance.

Minors

A parent or legal guardian should provide consent when the person using facial search is under the applicable age of consent.
