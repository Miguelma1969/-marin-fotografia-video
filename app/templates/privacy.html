Privacy Policy

Draft for launch preparation. Have a qualified attorney review this policy before public release, especially for biometric privacy requirements in every location where the service is offered.

Marin Fotografía y Video provides event photography, video, digital invitation, ordering, and optional facial-search services. This notice explains the categories of information the platform may process and the choices available to customers.

Information collected

  • Contact and order information, such as name, email, phone number, delivery address, products, payment status, and order history.
  • Event photographs and videos uploaded by the photographer.
  • A customer selfie submitted voluntarily for a one-time search within a selected event.
  • Technical information needed for security, fraud prevention, and reliable operation.

Facial search

Facial search is optional. The customer must provide express consent before the camera or uploaded selfie is used. The selfie is intended only to create a temporary comparison representation and search the selected event gallery. It is not intended for advertising, unrelated identification, or training a general-purpose AI model.

Retention and deletion

Temporary selfies should be deleted immediately after matching. Event images, orders, and transaction records may be retained as needed to deliver purchases, fulfill print orders, handle disputes, comply with accounting or legal duties, and protect the service. Customers may submit a deletion request from their private order page. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or necessary to complete an order.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate the platform, such as hosting, payment, email, storage, shipping, and printing providers. Information is not sold as a standalone customer list.

Security

The production service should use HTTPS, restricted administrative access, encrypted secrets, protected storage, activity logging, backups, and timely software updates. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

For events involving minors, the photographer or event organizer should obtain appropriate permission from a parent or legal guardian before enabling facial search or commercial distribution.
